# Legal, privacy and evidentiary notice

This public repository contains legal/investigative working notes. It is not a
court decision, official accusation, prosecutorial finding or substitute for
qualified legal advice.

## Evidence boundary

- Treat suspected misconduct, crimes, connections and motives as allegations
  or research hypotheses unless supported by an identified authoritative
  record.
- Distinguish source material, interpretation, allegation and adjudicated fact.
- Do not state that a person committed a crime merely because the person,
  agency or entity appears in a working map.
- Preserve source references, dates, document identifiers and correction
  history where allegations are discussed.

## Privacy and procedural boundary

Before publishing documents or extracts, review whether they contain personal
data, identifiers, addresses, health information, minors' data, protected
communications, sealed/restricted proceedings, confidential investigation
material or content subject to a court order. Redact or withhold material when
required by applicable law or judicial restriction.

## Licensing

No repository-wide open-source license was identified at the root during the
2026-09-16 audit. Public visibility does not itself grant a copyright license.
Third-party court records, photographs, reports, databases and other documents
retain their own legal status and cannot be relicensed merely by inclusion or
reference here.

## Corrections

Material later shown to be inaccurate should be corrected with a dated,
traceable update rather than silently converted from allegation into fact.
